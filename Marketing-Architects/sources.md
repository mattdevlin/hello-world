## Email Sequence Design

### Sequence Length & Timing
- **4-10 emails over 30 days** is the B2B best practice range
- **6 emails over 30 days** with 3-14 day spacing is optimal for construction/architecture industry
- Source: [Drip Campaign Best Practices](https://instantly.ai/blog/email-drip-campaign-best-practices/) — automated sequences see 80% higher open rates than single sends

### Behavioral Triggers
- Behavioral triggers outperform time-based sends by **497%**
- Source: [B2B Drip Campaign Guide](https://growleads.io/blog/b2b-drip-campaign-guide-to-boost-conversions/)

### CTA Performance
- Interest-based CTAs convert at **15%** vs **2%** for direct meeting requests (based on 304,000 email study)
- Source: [Interest-Based CTAs vs Meeting Requests Study](https://growleads.io/blog/interest-based-ctas-vs-meeting-requests-study/)

- Single CTA per email = **371% more clicks** than multiple CTAs
- Source: [CTA Best Practices](https://martal.ca/cta-best-practices-lb/)

- Personalized CTAs convert **202% better** than generic ones (based on 330,000 CTA study)
- Source: [HubSpot CTA Study](https://whitehat-seo.co.uk/blog/inbound-marketing-convert-calls-to-action)

### ROI
- Email marketing delivers **$42 ROI per $1 spent** in B2B
- Source: [B2B Email Marketing Statistics](https://stripo.email/blog/b2b-email-marketing-statistics-insights-to-shape-your-2025-strategy/)

## Architect-Specific Outreach

### Timing
- Best days: **Tuesday through Thursday**
- Best times: **9-11 AM and 2-4 PM** (recipient's local time)
- Architects prefer less frequent but more substantial emails
- Source: [Cold Email for Construction](https://www.abstraktmg.com/construction-cold-email-marketing/)

### Relationship-Driven Industry
- Architecture is a relationship-driven industry — many suppliers build their business around a core group of architect contacts who repeatedly specify their products
- Personalized emails consistently outperform mass outreach in open and click-through rates
- Source: [Email Marketing for Architecture Firms](https://www.abstraktmg.com/email-marketing-architecture-firms/)

### Segmentation
- Segment by: specialization (residential, commercial, sustainable), firm size (solo, mid-size, large), geographic focus, and climate zone
- Local architects prefer suppliers who understand regional building codes and climate considerations
- Source: [Building an Architect Email Database](https://scrap.io/building-an-architect-email-database-the-complete-b2b-guide)

## Property Value & Performance Premium

### NZ's First Passive House Sells $640K Above RV
- NZ's first certified Passive House (Kesteven Ave, Glendowie, Auckland) sold at auction for **$4.215M** — **$640K above its RV of $3.575M**
- Bidding started at $3.3M and climbed past $4M with 19 further bids after the auction was paused
- The 323m² home on a 999m² section was the first certified Passive House in the Southern Hemisphere
- **80% lower heating/cooling energy** than conventional builds
- Winter power bills no more than summer — energy costs a fraction of non-Passive House norms
- Only **~15% more to build** than a conventional home (owner's estimate)
- Won the **2015 South Pacific Passive House Award** and **2014 International Passive House Award**
- Source: [First Passive House in NZ sells at auction for top dollar](https://www.stuff.co.nz/home-property/360836312/watch-your-energy-costs-plummet-first-passive-house-listed-sale) (Stuff.co.nz)

## HubSpot Technical References

### Sequences vs Workflows
- **Sequences** (Sales Hub Pro+): 1-to-1 sales emails, enrollment via API, automatic unenrollment on reply
- **Workflows** (Marketing Hub Pro+): marketing emails, higher volume, event-triggered
- Source: [HubSpot Sequences vs Workflows](https://www.hublead.io/blog/hubspot-sequences-vs-workflows)

### API Limits
- HubSpot API rate limit: **100 requests per 10 seconds** per private app
- Batch API supports up to **100 records per request**
- Source: [HubSpot Sequences API Reference](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/automation-sequences-v4/guide)
